Already installed? Update just the effects

Use this only if your card already has an autorun/hs folder from this page (SampQOL 0.3.0). You only need the new effect files, and you do not need SampQOL again.

  1. Switch the MPC off, put the SD card in your computer and copy autorun/hs somewhere safe as a backup.
  3. Unzip it and copy everything in its hs folder into autorun/hs on the card, replacing the old files. Replace them all together.
  4. Eject the card, switch the MPC on and press B1.

SampQOL list styling is a setting too: it stays as it was on your card (add or delete autorun/hs/list-styling.on to turn it on or off). Stems is not in this update: to add Stems, run the installer above again with Add Stems ticked. These files are not part of the signed autorun, so nothing needs re-signing. If the MPC boots stock or restarts twice, put your backup of autorun/hs back.

Back up your SD card first. To undo: switch off and take the SD card out. Want your old setup back? Delete the new autorun folder and rename autorun-old back to autorun. Step-by-step help with pictures: install guide · FAQ · report a problem
