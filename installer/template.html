HenrySkillz MPC Sample Installer

Adds the HenrySkillz FX rack, EQ display and splash to SampQOL OS __SAMPQOL_VERSION__ on an MPC Sample (firmware 1.3.0). Everything happens in this page. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

  1. Download SampQOL-OS-v__SAMPQOL_VERSION__.zip from the official page: github.com/snarebes/SampQOL-OS/releases
  2. Drop that zip here (or, if your Mac already unzipped it, choose the SampQOL-OS-v__SAMPQOL_VERSION__ folder).
  3. Save the autorun-hs.zip this page gives you, unzip it, and copy the autorun folder to the top of your SD card.
