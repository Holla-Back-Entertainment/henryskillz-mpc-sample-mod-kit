HenrySkillz MPC Sample Installer

Adds the HenrySkillz FX rack (with HS Crush, Bass Focus and Tube Strip), track chains, key and BPM tags in the browser, LUFS meters, the EQ display, the SEQ desk page, the splash and, if you tick it, Stems to SampQOL OS __SAMPQOL_VERSION__ on an MPC Sample (firmware 1.3.0). Everything happens in this page. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

  1. Download SampQOL-OS-v__SAMPQOL_VERSION__.zip from the official page: github.com/snarebes/SampQOL-OS/releases
  2. Drop that zip here (or, if your Mac already unzipped it, choose the SampQOL-OS-v__SAMPQOL_VERSION__ folder).
  3. Save the autorun-hs.zip this page gives you, unzip it, and copy the autorun folder to the top of your SD card.

Already installed? Update just the effects

Your card already has an autorun/hs folder from this page. You only need the new effect files, and you do not need SampQOL again.

  1. Switch the MPC off, put the SD card in your computer and copy autorun/hs somewhere safe as a backup.
  3. Unzip it and copy everything in its hs folder into autorun/hs on the card, replacing the old files. Replace them all together.
  4. Eject the card, switch the MPC on and press B1.

Stems is not in this update: to add Stems, run the installer above again with Add Stems ticked. These files are not part of the signed autorun, so nothing needs re-signing. If the MPC boots stock or restarts twice, put your backup of autorun/hs back.

Back up your SD card first. To undo: switch off and take the SD card out, or delete the autorun folder from it. Step-by-step help with pictures: install guide · FAQ · report a problem
